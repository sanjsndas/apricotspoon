Baking as a
way of knowing.

We are a collective of bakers, writers, and flour-obsessed storytellers. Our mission is to document the craft—from the science of fermentation to the poetry of a perfect crust.

Our mission Meet the team →

Flour, water, salt, time — and something more.

At Apricot Spoon, we believe that baking is a form of slow knowledge. It connects us to tradition, to the seasons, and to each other. We publish guides, essays, and recipes that treat baking as both a science and an art. No shortcuts, no hype — just the honest craft of turning simple ingredients into something extraordinary.

Our story

2024

Apricot Spoon was founded in a small kitchen in London, with a single sourdough starter and a vision to create a new kind of baking publication.

2025

We published our first field guide, “The Baker’s Ratio,” and grew into a global community of home bakers, professionals, and flour enthusiasts.

2026

Today, we continue to explore the craft—from ancient grains to modern techniques—always with a spoonful of curiosity and a pinch of wonder.
