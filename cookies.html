Cookies Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Apricot Spoon Society

At Apricot Spoon Society, we use cookies to make your baking journey smoother — think of them as the digital equivalent of a perfectly greased pan. This policy explains what cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control them.

🍪 Our approach: We use only essential and performance cookies. No third‑party tracking for advertising. We respect your privacy as much as we respect a well‑proofed dough.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, understand how you interact with the content, and improve your overall experience.

How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: To keep the site functioning properly (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences).
  • Performance & analytics: To understand which recipes and articles are most popular, so we can create more of what you love.
  • Preferences: To remember your settings (like dark/light mode, if we ever add it).

We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or any form of cross‑site tracking.

Your consent

When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent to set non‑essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by adjusting your browser settings or by clicking the “Cookie settings” link (if available) in the footer.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can block or delete cookies entirely, but please note that some parts of our site may not function properly if you disable essential cookies.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Third‑party cookies

We use a minimal set of third‑party services:

  • Analytics (anonymized): We use a privacy‑friendly analytics tool that does not collect personal data. No cookies are used for cross‑site tracking.
  • Newsletter service: Our email provider may set a cookie to confirm subscription actions, but this is strictly functional.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, or any advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and we’ll update the “last updated” date accordingly.
