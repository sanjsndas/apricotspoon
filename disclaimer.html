Allergens & food safety

We clearly label common allergens in our recipes (e.g., gluten, dairy, nuts, eggs) whenever possible, but we cannot guarantee that all ingredients or cross‑contamination risks are identified. Always read product labels and be mindful of your own sensitivities.

  • Flour: We use all‑purpose and specialty flours. Substitute with gluten‑free blends at your own risk.
  • Eggs & dairy: Many recipes include eggs and butter. Plant‑based alternatives may work, but texture may vary.
  • Nuts & seeds: We often use almonds, walnuts, and sesame. If you have severe allergies, exercise caution.

Recipe testing & reliability

Every recipe on Apricot Spoon Society has been tested at least three times in our home kitchens. However, we are human — and ovens are unpredictable. If a recipe doesn’t work for you, we’d love to hear about it (and we might even re‑test it).

We encourage you to:

  • Read the entire recipe before starting.
  • Weigh ingredients (we provide weights and volumes).
  • Use an oven thermometer — your dial might lie.
  • Take notes: what worked, what didn’t, and what you’d tweak next time.
