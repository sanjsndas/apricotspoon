Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Apricot Spoon Society

At Apricot Spoon Society, we believe that privacy is as essential as a well‑fed sourdough starter. This policy explains how we handle your data when you visit our site, subscribe to our newsletter, or interact with our baking community.

📬 Our promise: We never sell your personal data. We only collect what’s necessary to serve you better — just like we only use the flour we need for the perfect loaf.

1. Information we collect

We collect limited information to improve your experience and to communicate with you about our content. This includes:

  • Email address — if you subscribe to our newsletter or comment on a post.
  • Usage data — anonymized analytics to understand which recipes and guides are most popular.
  • Cookies — to remember your preferences and to keep the site functioning smoothly. (See our Cookies Policy for details.)

2. How we use your information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • To send you our newsletter (only if you’ve opted in).
  • To improve our content based on what our readers engage with most.
  • To respond to your inquiries or comments.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our site.

3. Data storage & security

We take data protection seriously. Your information is stored securely and is only accessible to the core team at Apricot Spoon Society. We use industry‑standard encryption and regularly review our security practices.

4. Third‑party services

We use a few trusted third‑party services to power our site:

  • Analytics: We use anonymized analytics to understand how readers interact with our content. No personal data is shared.
  • Email newsletter: We use a reputable email service to send our newsletter. Your email address is stored with that provider, but we never share it with anyone else.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time (use the link at the bottom of every email).
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to delete your data — just get in touch.

6. Children’s privacy

Our site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we’ll notify you via our newsletter or by a notice on the site. The “last updated” date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, or if you’d like to exercise your data rights, please reach out to us at:

📧 privacy@apricotspoon.com

Or write to us at: Apricot Spoon Society, 42 Baker’s Lane, London, UK
